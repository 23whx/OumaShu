# Design System: OumaShu

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Station Board, in Ink and Vermilion"**

OumaShu is drawn as a railway network posted on a Japanese station wall. Every category is a line with one saturated colour and a two-letter code; every tool is a numbered station (IM 03). A 朱印 seal stamped with the Mincho 櫻 signs the network, and vermilion (朱) is the house colour. The map itself is the filter: searching or picking a line lights the stations that match and dims the rest. Wayfinding replaces the menu.

The frame is quiet so the lines can speak. The header is a translucent paper band with blur and a hairline rule, not a coloured sign. Surfaces are white and soft neutral grey, with neutral shadows; ink (#1c1c1e) appears only in the footer and the active chip. The type is signage: bold Overpass for names, Overpass Mono for codes, times and the status pill, Zen Kaku Gothic New for Japanese, and Shippori Mincho B1 only for the seal and the brand name. At night the board is lit: an ink ground, glowing lines, and sign plates that stay paper-white.

This world replaced the sakura-gradient look and then the navy sign band, which read as dated and corporate. Sakura (#e5608a) is now only the Image line.

**Key Characteristics:**
- 朱 is the brand: the seal, the station-name sign's band, focus rings, Open links and check marks.
- One colour per line, applied to badge frame, track, station ring, heading rule, chip code and card arrive pulse.
- Station-number badges: coloured rounded-square frame around a white plate with a dark bold code, identical in both themes.
- A light glass header with a hairline rule; ink is reserved for the footer and the active chip.
- Overpass Mono for codes, times, counts, domains and the status pill.
- The route map dims instead of hiding; the directory filters.
- Night mode is a lit board on ink, not an inverted page.

## Colors

Paper and soft neutrals, framed by hairlines, carrying twelve saturated line colours and one vermilion house colour.

### Primary
- **Shu Seal** (shu-seal; night shu-seal-night): the 朱印 seal and the station-name sign's neighbour band. White text on it holds 5.2:1 by day and 4.7:1 by night.
- **Shu** (shu; night shu-night): the brand accent. Focus outline, search focus ring and border, caret, selection tint (32%), the night glow of the station-name sign, quiet-button hover border.
- **Shu Ink** (shu-ink; night shu-ink-night): vermilion for text and small marks on paper: 櫻滿集 in the header, the card Open link, fact icons, the language check mark.
- **Shu on Ink** (shu-on-ink): 櫻滿集 on the ink footer, where shu-ink would fall to 2.7:1.

### Tertiary: the line palette
Each category owns exactly one colour and code, defined in `src/data/lines.ts` and passed to components as `--c`:
- **IM Image** (line-im-image), **VD Video** (line-vd-video), **AU Audio** (line-au-audio), **TX Text** (line-tx-text), **ED Education** (line-ed-education), **WS Workplace Slack** (line-ws-workplace-slack), **SR Search** (line-sr-search), **RS Resources** (line-rs-resources), **EN Entertainment** (line-en-entertainment), **SO Social** (line-so-social), **FT File Transfer** (line-ft-file-transfer), **GM Games** (line-gm-games).
- Lines are ordered biggest-first (IM, VD, AU, TX, ED, WS, SR, RS, EN, SO, FT, GM). A line without metadata falls back to the File Transfer slate.
- Line colours are the same in light and dark themes.
- SR Search (line-sr-search) sits within 4% lightness of shu at the same hue, so the two read as one red. Neither placement is confusable today (the seal and band carry no code; SR always appears with its code plate), but this is noted drift, not a pattern to repeat.

### Neutral
- **Paper** (paper; night paper-night): page ground, sticky chip rail ground, the night centre of station dots.
- **Header Glass** (header-glass; night header-glass-night): the translucent header band, always with `saturate(180%) blur(16px)` behind it.
- **Platform Panel** (platform-panel; night platform-panel-night): the route-map band, screenshot wells, language-menu hover.
- **Card** (card; night card-night): cards, chips, station dots, menus, the focused search field.
- **Ink** (ink; night ink-night): body text and the fixed dark text on every white plate.
- **Ink Band** (ink-band, ink-band-text, ink-band-mute; and night): the footer, the active chip, the skip link.
- **Muted Grey** (muted-grey; night muted-grey-night): lead paragraph, descriptions, station codes, counts, Japanese sub-names, search icon and placeholder, status pill text.
- **Rule Grey** (rule-grey; night rule-grey-night): 1px borders on cards, chips, menus and the map band; the dimmed track colour.
- **Hairline / Hairline Strong** (hairline, hairline-strong; and night): translucent rules in the header: its bottom edge, the search and status-pill borders, the `/` key and the pill's item dividers.
- **Wash / Wash Strong** (wash, wash-strong; and night): translucent fills in the header: search field and status pill at rest (wash), control hover (wash-strong).

### Named Rules
**The One Line, One Colour Rule.** A line colour appears only on things that belong to that line: its badge frame, track, station rings, the underline of its pressed label, the 3px rule under its directory heading, its chip code frame, its card hover border (45% mixed into rule grey) and its arrive pulse. Since sakura stepped down as the brand, no line shares a hex with the brand.

**The Shu Is The House Rule.** Vermilion belongs to OumaShu itself: the seal, the sign band, focus and the Open action. Brand meaning comes from those placements, not from the hue alone.

**The White Plate Rule.** Codes always sit as Ink on a white plate inside a coloured frame, in both themes. Badges, chip codes and the codes on the sign band never invert.

**The Quiet Frame Rule.** The header is glass and hairline, not a colour field. Ink fills only the footer and the active chip; content panels stay paper, platform grey or card.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Overpass, followed by the locale's CJK face (then PingFang SC, Microsoft YaHei, Segoe UI, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Overpass, same stack
**CJK Font (per locale, `--font-cjk`):** Zen Kaku Gothic New by default and for ja (with Hiragino Sans, Noto Sans JP); Noto Sans SC for zh-CN (with PingFang SC, Microsoft YaHei); Noto Sans TC for zh-TW (with PingFang TC, Microsoft JhengHei). Both Noto faces load at 400/500/700/900.
**Japanese Font (fixed, `--font-ja`):** Zen Kaku Gothic New (with Hiragino Sans, Noto Sans JP), for text that is Japanese in every locale
**Seal/Brand Font:** Shippori Mincho B1 800 (with Hiragino Mincho ProN, Yu Mincho, serif)
**Label/Mono Font:** Overpass Mono (with ui-monospace, SFMono-Regular, Consolas), tabular numerals

**Character:** Overpass is a highway-signage face: heavy, open and quick to read at a glance. Zen Kaku Gothic New gives Japanese names the weight of station kanji, and Noto Sans SC/TC give Chinese the correct glyph forms at matching weights. Shippori Mincho B1 brings the brushed, carved stroke of a seal to the 櫻 and the brand name. Overpass Mono turns codes, times and weather into timetable numerals.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp(2.4rem, 1.4rem + 3.2vw, 4rem), 1.04, -0.03em, max 15ch, balanced): the one hero headline.
- **Display CJK** (CJK face 900, clamp(2.1rem, 1.1rem + 2.6vw, 3.4rem), 1.2, +0.01em, max 13em): replaces Display when the UI is zh-CN, zh-TW or ja-JP. It is set in the locale face: Noto Sans SC, Noto Sans TC or Zen Kaku.
- **Station Name** (Zen Kaku 900, 64px, 1.1, +0.14em; 52px at ≤1180px): kanji on the station-name sign, with a 17px/600 romanisation tracked at +0.32em beneath it.
- **Brand Mincho** (Shippori Mincho B1 800, 15px, +0.06em): 櫻滿集 beside the wordmark in the header and footer.
- **Seal Glyph** (Shippori Mincho B1 800, 19px; 30px on the large seal): the single 櫻 in the seal.
- **Wordmark** (Overpass 800, 19px, -0.01em): "OumaShu".
- **Headline** (800, 26px, 1.1, -0.015em; 22px on phones): line headings in the directory, followed by the Japanese sub-name at 15px.
- **Title** (800, 17px, 1.25, -0.01em; 15.5px on phones): tool card names.
- **Lead** (400, 19px, 1.55, max 54ch; 17px on phones): hero sub-line, muted.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 1.5): card descriptions, clamped to two lines; footer text at 14px/1.65, max 62ch. The page base is 1.6.
- **Label** (600, 13.5px): chips. Line names on the map are 800/15px (13px on phones); station names are 700/12.5px, balanced and clamped to three lines.
- **Badge Code** (800, 12px, +0.02em; 15px in large badges, 10-11px in compact and inline badges).
- **Mono** (Overpass Mono 500-600, 9-13px, tabular): the status pill (12px; 11.5px on phones), station codes (10.5px; 9px on phones), counts, domains, the footer base line.

### Named Rules
**The Mono Is For Readouts Rule.** Overpass Mono sets codes, times, temperatures, counts and domains, plus the status pill as one readout. Names and sentences elsewhere never use it.

**The Mincho Is For The Seal Rule.** Shippori Mincho B1 sets only the seal's 櫻 and the 櫻滿集 brand name. Headings, the station-name kanji and Japanese sub-names stay in Zen Kaku Gothic New.

**The Right Glyphs Rule.** Chinese is never set in a Japanese face. Everything that follows the UI language goes through the per-locale CJK face: body, headings, the CJK hero headline and the typographic card plate. Zen Kaku (fixed) is reserved for text that is Japanese in every locale: the line sub-names and the 櫻滿集 sign kanji. Shippori Mincho stays on the seal and the brand name.

**The Locale Line-break Rule.** Station names are balanced in every locale. In ja they break by phrase (`word-break: auto-phrase; line-break: strict`). In zh-CN and zh-TW they break at the spaces between Latin and CJK runs and split characters only when they must (`word-break: keep-all; overflow-wrap: anywhere; line-break: strict`).

**The Japanese Sub-name Rule.** Line names carry their Japanese name in Zen Kaku 500, muted, beside or under the local name. When the UI is Japanese, the sub-name is hidden because it would repeat, and the hero headline and lead also break by phrase.

## Layout

A centred 1320px container with 32px gutters (16px on phones). From the top:
- A sticky 64px glass header: seal and wordmark; search capped at 560px; the status pill pushed right; language and theme controls; 28px between groups.
- A hero grid with the headline on the left and the 400px station-name sign on the right, 56px apart.
- The full-width route map band on platform grey.
- A sticky chip rail directly under the header.
- The directory: one section per line, 64px apart (44px on phones). Cards sit on an auto-fill grid of minmax(292px, 1fr) with 22px gaps (12px on phones).

**The route map.** Lines sit in two columns 48px apart. A line with more than three stations spans both columns as a full-width line. Each line row is a 192px label column (170px at ≤960px) plus its tracks. Stations share equal columns within a track, capped at 150px each so short lines stay short.

**Wrapping lines.** A track holds at most 7 stations. Longer lines (Image, 14 stations) stack further tracks 4px apart. Every track except the last runs its bar to the right edge, and the next track starts its bar at the left edge, so the line reads as continuing.

**Responsive rules.**
- ≤1180px: the station-name sign narrows to 340px and its kanji to 52px.
- ≤960px: the hero stacks (sign up to 400px wide), the map drops to one column, and the search pushes right.
- ≤760px:
  - **Header:** it stops sticking and wraps: seal and controls, then a full-width search, then the status pill at 11.5px.
  - **Hero:** the station-name sign is removed.
  - **Compact phone map:** two columns, 14px apart, with every line half-width. Badges shrink to 26px, and Japanese sub-names and station names drop out. Stations become 16px dots with 9px mono codes, capped at 46px each and still 7 per track. Dimming still applies.
  - **Chips and cards:** the chip rail sticks to the top of the viewport. Cards turn horizontal: the screenshot is 38% wide (max 168px, min 112px tall) beside the body.
- **The chip rail** is a single row at every width. It scrolls sideways with no visible scrollbar and fades out over its last 56px.
- Scroll padding is 132px (72px on phones) so anchored sections clear the sticky bars.

## Elevation & Depth

Mostly flat. Depth comes from translucency and tone first: a glass header over the page, a platform-grey map band, white cards, an ink footer. Neutral black shadows come second, in two steps. Cards sit on `card` and rise to `lift` on hover.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.05), 0 6px 18px -8px rgba(0,0,0,.16)`; night `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,.4), 0 8px 22px -10px rgba(0,0,0,.6)`): resting cards and the station-name sign.
- **Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 4px rgba(0,0,0,.06), 0 18px 36px -14px rgba(0,0,0,.28)`; night `0 2px 6px rgba(0,0,0,.45), 0 22px 40px -16px rgba(0,0,0,.75)`): hovered cards, the language menu.
- **Seal frame** (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 0 2.5px` shu-seal, `inset 0 0 0 3.5px rgba(255,255,255,.9)`; large `4px` / `5.5px`): the thin white inner frame of the 朱印.
- **Shu focus ring** (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px` shu-seal at 22%): the search field while focused.
- **Line glow, night only** (track `0 0 12px -1px` line colour at 75%; dot `0 0 10px -1px` at 80%): the lit-board glow. Removed from dimmed lines and stations.

### Named Rules
**The Glass Header Rule.** The header is paper at 72% (ink at 70% by night) over `saturate(180%) blur(16px)`, closed by a single hairline. Its fields and readouts sit on wash with hairline borders; nothing in it is a solid colour except the seal.

**The Lit Board Rule.** In dark mode the page is a lit sign, not an inverted one:
- The ground goes ink (paper-night).
- Tracks and station rings glow in their line colour, station dots take the ink ground as their centre, and station names go pure white.
- The station-name sign stays a paper plate (#f7f7f7) with a soft vermilion halo, and its 朱 band keeps white text.
- The active chip inverts to a white plate with ink text.
- Screenshots dim to `brightness(.78) saturate(.9)` and return to full on hover.

## Shapes

Squares for the seal, rounded squares for signs, circles for stations, pills for chips and readouts:
- **Seal:** a near-square stamp (3px radius; 4px large).
- **Badges:** frames are 8px (10px large) around a 5px (6px) inner plate.
- **Controls and fields:** controls are 8px and the search field 10px.
- **Cards and sign:** cards and the station-name sign are 12px (cards 10px on phones).
- **Pills:** chips, the status pill and tags are full pills.
- **Tracks:** 6px bars with 3px ends (4px on phones).
- **Station dots:** 22px rings with a 4.5px line-coloured border (16px with 3.5px on phones).
- **Rules:** borders are 1px rule grey on content and 1px hairline in the header. The only heavy strokes are the 3px line-colour rule under each directory heading and the 3px line-colour underline on a hovered or pressed line label.
- **Icons:** inline SVG with a 1.8px round-capped stroke (2.2-2.4px for small emphasis), never filled.

## Components

### 朱印 Seal (signature)
The brand mark, stamped beside the wordmark in the header and footer, and large on the station-name sign.
- **Shape:** a 32px vermilion square (3px radius) with a white Shippori Mincho 櫻 at 19px. A white inner frame sits 2.5px in (90% white, 1px). **Large** is 52px (4px radius) with a 30px glyph and the frame 4px in.
- **Colour:** shu-seal in both themes (its night value is slightly brighter). It never takes a line colour.

### Header
- **Band:** glass (see Elevation), sticky, 64px, hairline bottom rule.
- **Brand:** the seal, "OumaShu" at 800/19px, and 櫻滿集 in Brand Mincho using shu-ink (shu-on-ink in the footer).
- **Status pill:** location, time and weather in one mono readout. It has a wash fill, a hairline border and a full pill radius with 4px padding. Items are 9px apart with hairline-strong dividers between them. Text is muted, with the clock in ink 600 and a 15px stroke weather icon.
- **Controls:** 38px transparent buttons with 8px radius in inherited ink; hover fills wash-strong. The language menu is a card with the Lift shadow, a 10px radius and a shu-ink check on the current locale.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Search:** 38px high, 10px radius, wash fill, hairline border, ink text (500/15px). An 18px muted stroke icon sits at the left and a `/` key hint at the right (hairline-strong border, 6px radius), which hides on focus. The `/` key focuses the field from anywhere; Esc clears it.
- **Focus:** the fill turns card and the border turns shu-seal, with a 3px shu ring at 22%.

### Station Badge
The core sign element. It frames a line or tool code.
- **Shape:** a coloured rounded square (34px, 8px radius, 3px frame) holding a white plate (5px radius) with an 800-weight 12px code in Ink.
- **Variants:**
  - **Large:** 44px, 10px radius, 4px frame, 15px code.
  - **Inline (on cards):** 26px tall, auto-width, 2.5px frame, 11px code like "IM 03".
  - **Compact (phone map):** 26px, 2px frame, 10px code.
- **Colour:** the frame takes the line colour. The plate and text never change with the theme.

### Route Map Track and Station
- **Track:** a 6px line-coloured bar at the station-dot centre, running from the first station to the middle of the last. A track that continues on the next row runs its bar to the edge. Tracks draw in left to right on load: clip-path, 1.1s, cubic-bezier(.16,1,.3,1), staggered 60ms per line after 120ms, and only when motion is allowed.
- **Station:** a ring dot, with a 12.5px/700 name below it and a muted mono code under that.
  - **Hover, focus, or its card hovered:** the dot scales to 1.3 and fills solid with the line colour (260ms).
  - **Dimmed (no match):** opacity .3 with a muted ring.
  - **Line with no lit stations:** its track greys to rule grey and its label drops to .4.
- **Line label:** a button holding the badge plus the name (800/15px) and Japanese sub-name. On hover or when pressed (`aria-pressed`), the name gets a 3px underline in the line colour. Pressing filters the directory to that line and scrolls there.
- **Click a station:** scrolls its card to the centre, focuses it and plays the arrive pulse.

### Chips (line filter rail)
- **Style:** 34px pill, 1px rule-grey border, card ground, 600/13.5px label. Each chip holds a mono code plate (2px line-coloured frame, white plate, Ink), the line name and a muted mono count. "All lines" has no code plate.
- **Hover:** the border takes the line colour (ink for "All lines").
- **On:** an ink-band fill and border with white text, and the count at .7. At night it inverts to a near-white plate with ink text. One chip is on at a time; pressing an active line chip returns to all.

### Cards (station cards)
- **Corner Style:** 12px (10px on phones).
- **Background:** card, with a 16:10 screenshot well (platform grey) cropped from the top. A tool without a screenshot gets a typographic plate: its name in the locale CJK face at 900 and 30px, the line colour mixed 70% into ink, on the line colour at 14% over platform grey.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Card at rest, Lift on hover (see Elevation).
- **Border:** 1px rule grey. On hover it takes the line colour at 45%.
- **Internal Padding:** 16px 18px (12px 14px on phones).
- **Content:** the inline badge and title, then a two-line muted description. The meta row holds the mono domain (with ellipsis), an optional third-party pill tag, and a shu-ink "Open" label with an arrow.
- **Hover:** the card rises 3px (420ms), the screenshot scales to 1.035 (700ms) and the arrow nudges 2px up and right. Cards do not rise on phones.
- **Arrive:** after a station click, a 5px ring in the line colour blooms and fades over 1.6s, settling back to Card.
- **Counts:** line counts and search results use singular strings for one ("1 station", "Found 1 tool").

### Station-name Sign (home hero)
- A white sign carrying the large seal at top left, with the kanji name and romanisation centred.
- Its shu-seal lower band links ← to the first line and → to the last. Each link pairs a code plate with white 800/14px text, in both themes.

### Footer (terminus)
- A 6px stripe of every line colour in map order, over an ink-band footer.
- The brand is repeated with the seal and 櫻滿集 in shu-on-ink.
- Headings are ink-band-text 800/14px and body text is ink-band-mute.

### Global focus
- A 2px shu outline at a 3px offset with a 4px radius.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** give every new category a code, one colour from the line palette and a Japanese name in `src/data/lines.ts`, and drive its UI through `--c`.
- **Do** put codes on white plates with Ink text inside a coloured frame, in both themes.
- **Do** set locale-following CJK text in the per-locale face (`--font-cjk`); keep Zen Kaku (`--font-ja`) for always-Japanese text.
- **Do** sign the brand with the 朱印 seal and 櫻滿集 in Shippori Mincho B1; keep Mincho to those two marks.
- **Do** build header pieces from wash fills and hairline borders over the glass band.
- **Do** keep Overpass Mono for codes, times, counts, domains and the status pill, with tabular numerals.
- **Do** dim map stations (.3) and lines (.4, grey track) instead of removing them; hide only directory cards.
- **Do** wrap long lines at 7 stations per track and run a continuing track to the edge.
- **Do** guard decorative motion with `prefers-reduced-motion` and use the house ease `cubic-bezier(.16, 1, .3, 1)` (200ms for state changes).
- **Do** make dark mode a lit board: ink ground, glowing line colours, white sign plates, dimmed screenshots that brighten on hover.
- **Do** draw icons as inline SVG with a 1.8px round stroke.

### Don't:
- **Don't** bring back a solid coloured header band; the navy sign band was retired as dated and corporate.
- **Don't** bring back sakura gradients, the leaf-green accent or animated gradient fills.
- **Don't** use sakura #e5608a as the brand; it is the Image line only.
- **Don't** use line colours for running text or fills behind text; they are frames, tracks, rings and rules.
- **Don't** give two categories the same line colour, and don't pick a new line colour in the vermilion family.
- **Don't** set shu-ink on the ink band (2.7:1); use shu-on-ink there.
- **Don't** set Simplified or Traditional Chinese in Zen Kaku Gothic New; its Japanese glyph forms and missing simplified glyphs drop text to the system font.
- **Don't** use emoji or icon-font glyphs for icons.
